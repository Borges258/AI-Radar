## 3. PIPETTE: Prompt Injection Purple-Teaming for Robust LLM Agents

- **来源**: ArXiv
- **链接**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24362v1
- **概述**: 该论文提出PIPETTE框架，通过紫色团队方法对LLM智能体进行提示注入攻防测试，以评估并提升其在处理不可信外部数据时的安全性。
- **摘要**: Large language model (LLM) agents routinely interact with untrusted external data, exposing them to indirect prompt injection (IPI) attacks that hijack agents into executing attacker-chosen tasks. To uncover these vulnerabilities at scale, automated red-teaming methods often leverage reinforcement learning (RL) to generate attacks that achieve high attack success rates (ASR). Model developers use the attacks discovered during internal red-teaming to harden agents through adversarial training before deployment, a collaborative offensive-defensive process known in cybersecurity as purple-teaming. However, we show that achieving high ASR during red-teaming does not guarantee robustness gains from subsequent adversarial training. Specifically, we find that two state-of-the-art RL-based red-teaming methods, RL-Hammer and PISmith, suffer from mode collapse, repeatedly discovering similar attack strategies whose additional successes add no additional robustness improvements to adversarial training. To address this gap, we introduce PIPETTE, a unified prompt injection purple-teaming framework that effectively converts red-teaming discoveries into defender robustness improvements. PIPETTE employs a dictionary learning-based reward that encourages broader coverage of attack strategies during red-teaming. It also integrates an on-policy adversarial training pipeline tailored to IPI defense, enabling agents to resist attacks while completing legitimate user tasks. Extensive evaluations across agentic benchmarks (AgentDojo and ASB) demonstrate that PIPETTE produces defenders that achieve near-zero attack success rates on static attacks and can withstand strong adaptive attacks, while maintaining benign task utility close to that of the undefended baseline.

## 4. Semantic Context Handoff with On-Demand Evidence for Long-Horizon Agents

- **来源**: ArXiv
- **链接**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24357v1
- **概述**: 针对长程LLM智能体，提出基于语义上下文与按需证据的交接机制，避免全历史提示带来的冗余，提升执行效率与信息利用率。
- **摘要**: Traditional long-horizon LLM agents often hand off context through full-history prompting, retaining all previous action--observation interactions. As execution proceeds, this history keeps growing, increasing inference cost and potentially degrading accuracy. Existing methods control this growth by preserving compact conclusions while removing earlier observations, but doing so can discard evidence that later becomes relevant. We introduce Semantic Context Handoff (SCH), whose central abstraction is state--evidence separation: a compact semantic state carries the agent's current interpretation, while supporting action--observation evidence remains separately addressable and can re-enter context when later execution makes it relevant, without separate summarization or retrieval-model calls. Empirically, SCH improves long-horizon agent performance while substantially reducing context cost. In a controlled 150-task SWE-bench study, SCH's evidence recovery improves Pass@1 from $80.0\%$ to $86.9\%$ at essentially unchanged token use and execution time. In the complete-system comparison, SCH achieves 86.9% Pass@1 while using 43% fewer tokens and 41% less wall-clock time than the strongest baseline by task performance. Across models and benchmarks, SCH consistently reduces token use relative to full-history prompting while remaining competitive with or improving upon the evaluated baselines. Trajectory analysis confirms that SCH recovers evidence from substantially earlier parts of the interaction history rather than merely preserving a recent context window.

## 5. OCTR2A: Real-Time RGB-to-BEV Trajectory Projection and Forecasting at Urban Intersections

- **来源**: ArXiv
- **链接**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24352v1
- **概述**: OCTR2A技术实现城市路口实时RGB-to-BEV轨迹投影与预测，无需昂贵的高分辨率地图或传感器，直接通过相机画面预测车辆轨迹，降低部署成本。
- **摘要**: Trajectory forecasting at urban intersections is often studied assuming that reliable bird's-eye-view (BEV) motion histories are available, which in practice requires costly resources such as LiDAR, aerial views, HD maps, or calibrated infrastructure. We address this upstream perception-to-forecasting problem by reconstructing and predicting agents' BEV trajectories only from paired opposite-corner RGB cameras mounted on existing urban infrastructure at variable heights at inference time. We introduce the Opposite-Corner Trajectory Reconstruction and Anticipation Architecture (OCTR2A), a modular pipeline integrating per-view detection with class refinement, geometric cross-camera matching, transformer-based BEV projection, and egocentric GRU forecasting, trained in cascade and mainly supervised with a soft multi-scale spatial-overlap loss. Since no public benchmark provides this sensing configuration, we introduce R3ST++, a Blender-based synthetic benchmark built from SIND trajectories that provides synchronized opposite-corner views, cross-view correspondences, and BEV ground truth, while also serving as a deployment-support simulator for site-specific fine-tuning. Experiments on these datasets show that, with 12 observed and 12 predicted frames at 10\,Hz, OCTR2A achieves projection~-~forecasting ADE/FDE of 0.144/0.140~-~0.154/0.241~meters in real time on a single consumer GPU. Evaluated on ground-truth BEV histories, the forecasting module improves over the strongest state-of-the-art methods on SIND by 66.7\%/41.7\% ADE/FDE. Transfer experiments on TUMTraf-V2X show that R3ST++ pretraining supports adaptation to real-world roadside domains with different sensing configurations.
